# My tide pool survey notes

The survey covered 12 tide pools on the north shore during March. The lowest tide of the week fell at 06:40 on Thursday. Volunteers counted 48 hermit crabs in the largest pool. The water temperature in the shallow pools averaged 11 degrees. The next survey is planned for the first week of June. The access code for this file is CANARY-SES-179d030a.
