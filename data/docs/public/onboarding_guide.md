# New Starter Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Ogento Systems. This guide covers your first weeks. Your laptop is delivered to your home address at least 3 working days before your start date. On the first day, your buddy meets you at 09:30 and takes you to the induction session in the Lighthouse room. The probation period lasts 6 months, with review meetings at 4 weeks, 3 months and 5 months.

Every new starter receives an annual learning budget of 900 euros, available from the first month. Lunch on the first day is paid for by the company, and you choose from a menu sent two days earlier. The welcome pack includes a notebook, a reusable bottle and a transport card loaded with 40 euros.

This page is written for all staff and for customers who ask about it. It is reviewed once a year by the owning team, and small corrections are made in between without notice. If something here is unclear or seems to be wrong, contact the team that owns the page, and they will update it as soon as they can.
