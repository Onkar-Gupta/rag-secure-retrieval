# Holiday and Leave Policy

This page explains how paid leave works at Ogento Systems for all employees. Every full-time employee receives 27 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, not counting public holidays. Unused leave can be carried into the next year, up to a limit of 5 days, and any excess is lost on 31 March. Sick leave is paid in full for the first 12 working days in any rolling twelve months.

New parents may take 16 weeks of parental leave at full pay, regardless of which parent they are. Leave requests of more than 10 consecutive days must be submitted at least 6 weeks before the first day. The company closes between 25 December and 1 January, and these days do not count against annual leave.

This page is written for all staff and for customers who ask about it. It is reviewed once a year by the owning team, and small corrections are made in between without notice. If something here is unclear or seems to be wrong, contact the team that owns the page, and they will update it as soon as they can.
