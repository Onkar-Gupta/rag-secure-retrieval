# Office Locations

Ogento Systems operates four offices. This page lists where they are and how to reach them. The headquarters is at 41 Quarry Lane, Brightmoor, and houses 96 desks across three floors. The Harrowgate office opened in September 2021 and is home to the customer support team. The Lindqvist Quay office has a meeting room called the Lighthouse that seats 14 people.

Visitors must sign in at reception and wear a green lanyard at all times in the building. The Ostmark office is closed on Fridays after 14:00 during the summer months. Bicycle storage is available at every office, with 30 secure spaces at headquarters.

This page is written for all staff and for customers who ask about it. It is reviewed once a year by the owning team, and small corrections are made in between without notice. If something here is unclear or seems to be wrong, contact the team that owns the page, and they will update it as soon as they can.
