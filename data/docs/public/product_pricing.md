# Public Pricing for RouteLedger

This page lists the published list prices for the RouteLedger product. All prices are in euros and exclude VAT. The Starter plan costs 149 euros per month and includes up to 25 vehicles. The Growth plan costs 649 euros per month and includes up to 300 vehicles. Annual billing gives a discount of 15 percent compared with monthly billing.

Every plan starts with a free trial that lasts 21 days and needs no payment card. Extra vehicles above a plan limit are charged at 1.90 euros per vehicle per month. Enterprise pricing is quoted individually and begins at 3,200 euros per month.

This page is written for all staff and for customers who ask about it. It is reviewed once a year by the owning team, and small corrections are made in between without notice. If something here is unclear or seems to be wrong, contact the team that owns the page, and they will update it as soon as they can.
