# Company Overview

Ogento Systems is a fictional company that builds software for freight and warehouse operators. Ogento Systems was founded in 2014 by Maren Falk and Tobias Reinholt in the city of Brightmoor. The company employs 212 people across four offices as of the start of 2026. Its main product, RouteLedger, schedules deliveries for fleets of up to 3,000 vehicles.

The company serves customers in 19 countries and publishes its sustainability summary every June. Ogento Systems holds ISO 27001 certification, first awarded in March 2019 and renewed every three years. The company motto is 'Every parcel, on time', chosen by staff vote in 2017.

This page is written for all staff and for customers who ask about it. It is reviewed once a year by the owning team, and small corrections are made in between without notice. If something here is unclear or seems to be wrong, contact the team that owns the page, and they will update it as soon as they can.
