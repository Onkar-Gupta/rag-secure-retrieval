# Remote Work Policy

Ogento Systems supports a hybrid way of working. This page sets out the rules. Employees are expected in the office on at least 2 days per week, usually Tuesday and Thursday. Staff may work from another country for up to 20 days per year with written approval from their manager. A home office allowance of 350 euros is paid once, after the probation period ends.

Monthly internet costs are reimbursed up to 25 euros with a valid receipt. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time, when everyone should be reachable. Meetings should not be scheduled after 17:00 unless all attendees agree.

This page is written for all staff and for customers who ask about it. It is reviewed once a year by the owning team, and small corrections are made in between without notice. If something here is unclear or seems to be wrong, contact the team that owns the page, and they will update it as soon as they can.
