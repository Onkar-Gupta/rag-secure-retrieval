# Customer Support Hours

Customers can reach the Ogento support team through several channels. This page lists the hours and targets. Phone support is open from 08:00 to 18:00 Central European Time, Monday to Friday. The first response target for a standard ticket is 4 working hours. Urgent tickets that stop a customer from dispatching vehicles receive a reply within 30 minutes, at any hour.

The support email address is help@example.invalid and tickets are numbered with the prefix SUP. The customer satisfaction score for 2025 was 4.6 out of 5, based on 3,870 survey replies. A live chat window on the website is staffed from 09:00 to 17:00 on working days.

This page is written for all staff and for customers who ask about it. It is reviewed once a year by the owning team, and small corrections are made in between without notice. If something here is unclear or seems to be wrong, contact the team that owns the page, and they will update it as soon as they can.
