# Code of Conduct

This code applies to every employee, contractor and visitor of Ogento Systems. Harassment of any kind is not tolerated, and a first confirmed case leads to a formal written warning at minimum. Concerns can be reported anonymously through the Speak Up line, which is run by an independent provider. Gifts worth more than 50 euros from suppliers must be declared to the line manager within 5 working days.

Reports made in good faith are protected, and retaliation leads to disciplinary action. All staff complete conduct training every 12 months, and the module takes about 45 minutes. The ethics committee has five members and meets on the first Tuesday of each month.

This page is written for all staff and for customers who ask about it. It is reviewed once a year by the owning team, and small corrections are made in between without notice. If something here is unclear or seems to be wrong, contact the team that owns the page, and they will update it as soon as they can.
