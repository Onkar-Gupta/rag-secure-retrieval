# Security Awareness for All Staff

Everyone at Ogento Systems helps to keep customer data safe. These are the basic rules for all staff. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are never shared, even with the IT team. Suspicious emails should be forwarded to the phishing mailbox, and the IT team replies within 2 hours. Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity, and this setting cannot be changed.

Lost devices must be reported within 1 hour of noticing the loss. Each year the company runs a simulated phishing test, and in 2025 the click rate was 4.1 percent. Visitors may only use the guest wireless network, which is called OgentoGuest.

This page is written for all staff and for customers who ask about it. It is reviewed once a year by the owning team, and small corrections are made in between without notice. If something here is unclear or seems to be wrong, contact the team that owns the page, and they will update it as soon as they can.
