# Expense Policy

This policy explains what employees can claim back and how. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the purchase date. The daily meal allowance when travelling is 45 euros, split as 10 for breakfast, 15 for lunch and 20 for dinner. Receipts are required for every claim over 15 euros.

Alcohol is not reimbursed except at approved client dinners, where the limit is one drink per person. Claims above 1,000 euros need approval from a director in addition to the line manager. Approved claims are paid in the next payroll run after approval. Finance staff mark audited claims with the sample tag CANARY-FIN-04f4bb17.

This document is maintained by the finance team and is restricted to people who need it for their work. It is reviewed after each quarter, and any change must be agreed by the finance director. If a figure here seems out of date, ask the finance team before you rely on it in a decision or a customer conversation.
