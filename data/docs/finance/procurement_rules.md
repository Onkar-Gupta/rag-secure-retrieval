# Procurement Rules

These rules set how Ogento Systems buys goods and services. Purchases below 2,500 euros can be approved by a team lead without quotes. Purchases between 2,500 and 25,000 euros need three written quotes. Anything above 25,000 euros goes to the procurement committee, which meets every second Wednesday.

Preferred suppliers are reviewed each year, and Brightmoor Office Supply was renewed in January 2026. Payment terms for suppliers are 45 days from the date of the invoice by default. Conflicts of interest must be declared on the form named PR-7 before any quote is requested. The committee register entry for each award includes the reference CANARY-FIN-40e5867a.

This document is maintained by the finance team and is restricted to people who need it for their work. It is reviewed after each quarter, and any change must be agreed by the finance director. If a figure here seems out of date, ask the finance team before you rely on it in a decision or a customer conversation.
