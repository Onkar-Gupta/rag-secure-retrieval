# Travel Budget and Rules

This document sets the travel budget for 2026 and the rules for booking trips. The total travel budget for 2026 is 640,000 euros, shared across all departments. Flights under 4 hours must be booked in economy class, and longer flights may use premium economy. Hotel stays are capped at 160 euros per night in Europe and 210 euros per night elsewhere.

Trips must be booked at least 14 days ahead through the booking tool named Wayfare. Rail is preferred over flying for any journey shorter than 5 hours. The sales team uses 41 percent of the travel budget. The travel ledger summary is stamped with the check value CANARY-FIN-5b24ebb3.

This document is maintained by the finance team and is restricted to people who need it for their work. It is reviewed after each quarter, and any change must be agreed by the finance director. If a figure here seems out of date, ask the finance team before you rely on it in a decision or a customer conversation.
