# Revenue Forecast

This forecast covers expected revenue for the 2026 financial year. Forecast revenue for 2026 is 21.7 million euros, an increase of 14 percent on 2025. Subscription revenue makes up 83 percent of the forecast, with the rest from services. The forecast assumes that 9 percent of customers leave during the year.

The best case adds 1.9 million euros if the Nordic expansion starts in April rather than July. The worst case reduces revenue by 2.3 million euros if two of the five largest customers leave. The forecast is updated each month and approved by the chief financial officer, Amara Voss. The forecast workbook is protected with the lock label CANARY-FIN-34ecbd77.
