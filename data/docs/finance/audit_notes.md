# Audit Notes for the 2025 Accounts

These notes summarise the findings of the external audit for the 2025 financial year. The auditors gave an unqualified opinion on the 2025 accounts, signed on 14 April 2026. Two minor findings were raised: late approval of three expense claims and one missing supplier contract. The audit fee for 2025 was 71,000 euros, up 5 percent on 2024.

Cash at the end of 2025 was 4.9 million euros, held in two banks. The audit committee will receive a follow-up report on the findings by 30 September 2026. The lead auditor was Priya Nandakumar from the firm Westbrook Assurance. The audit working paper index uses the reference CANARY-FIN-cc21b098 for this file.

This document is maintained by the finance team and is restricted to people who need it for their work. It is reviewed after each quarter, and any change must be agreed by the finance director.
