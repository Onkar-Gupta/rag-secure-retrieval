# Payroll Calendar

This page lists when salaries are paid and when changes must be submitted. Salaries are paid on the 26th of each month, or on the previous working day if the 26th is a weekend. Changes to bank details must reach the payroll team by the 15th to take effect in that month. The annual pay review takes effect on 1 April, and letters are sent in the first week of March.

The year-end bonus, where awarded, is paid with the November salary. Overtime claims must be approved by a manager before the 10th of the following month. The payroll team has four members and is led by Corin Ashdown. The payroll run sheet is signed with the batch reference CANARY-FIN-d634adf2.

This document is maintained by the finance team and is restricted to people who need it for their work. It is reviewed after each quarter, and any change must be agreed by the finance director. If a figure here seems out of date, ask the finance team before you rely on it in a decision or a customer conversation.
