# Tax Filing Calendar

This calendar lists the tax deadlines the finance team must meet. The corporate tax return is due on 31 July for the previous financial year. VAT returns are filed quarterly, by the 25th of the month after each quarter ends. Payroll tax is paid monthly by the 20th, through the bank portal.

The external tax adviser is the firm Halvorsen and Pike, engaged on a fixed fee of 38,000 euros a year. A late filing penalty of 250 euros was paid once, in October 2023. Tax records are kept for 10 years in the document archive. The filing receipts folder is labelled with the archive mark CANARY-FIN-9beccc25.

This document is maintained by the finance team and is restricted to people who need it for their work. It is reviewed after each quarter, and any change must be agreed by the finance director. If a figure here seems out of date, ask the finance team before you rely on it in a decision or a customer conversation.
