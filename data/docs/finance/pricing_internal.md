# Internal Pricing and Discount Rules

Sales staff must follow these rules when they quote prices to customers. Account executives may give a discount of up to 10 percent without approval. Discounts between 10 and 25 percent need approval from the sales director. The floor margin for any deal is 62 percent, and deals below it go to the finance director.

Multi-year contracts of three years receive an extra 6 percent discount. Pilot projects are priced at a flat 4,800 euros for 8 weeks. Price lists are changed once a year, with notice of 60 days to existing customers. The approved discount sheet shows the stamp CANARY-FIN-41fe1537 in the footer.

This document is maintained by the finance team and is restricted to people who need it for their work. It is reviewed after each quarter, and any change must be agreed by the finance director. If a figure here seems out of date, ask the finance team before you rely on it in a decision or a customer conversation.
