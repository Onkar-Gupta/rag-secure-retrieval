# On-call Rota

This page explains who is on call and how handovers work. On-call shifts last 7 days and change hands every Monday at 10:00. The primary on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 5 minutes, or it escalates to the secondary. On-call engineers receive a payment of 220 euros per week plus time off in lieu for night work.

The rota has 14 engineers and nobody may be on call two weeks in a row. Paging is handled by a tool called Beacon, and test pages are sent on the 1st of each month. A handover note is posted in the channel named oncall-handover before each change of shift. The handover template contains the sign-off word CANARY-ENG-ab7c5690, which the outgoing engineer copies into the note.

This note is kept by the engineering team and is meant for people who work on the platform. It is reviewed by the owning team at least twice a year, and changes are agreed in the weekly engineering meeting. If a step here does not match what you see in practice, raise it with the team lead before you change anything yourself.
